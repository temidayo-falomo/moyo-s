How clearance works

Four steps replace the physical queue at the faculty office.

1

Payment is confirmed

School-fee status is read from the university portal by the server. Students cannot edit it.

2

Student submits a request

The student attaches the fee receipt, passport photograph and student ID card, then signs a declaration.

3

Faculty officer approves

Officers search, filter and approve eligible requests individually or in batches within a scheduled window.

4

Invigilator verifies

At the hall, the QR code is scanned and checked against the live server record before the student is admitted.

Designed around the university record

The clearance document is a pointer to the record, never the proof itself.

Students see only their own record

Every request will be tied to the signed-in student on the server. There is no way to look up another student.

Unique verification tokens

Each issued clearance will carry an unpredictable token generated by the backend. Scanning it retrieves the authoritative record.

Authorized approvals, fully logged

Only authorized staff can approve, revoke or verify. Every action is written to an audit log.
