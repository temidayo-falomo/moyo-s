      How your record is protected

      • You can only see your own record. There is no option to view another student.
      • Your school-fee status is read-only. It will be checked by the server against the official university portal.
      • Clearance is issued only after an authorized faculty officer approves your request.
      • At the hall, the invigilator checks your QR code against the live university record. A printout or screenshot alone is not proof.
